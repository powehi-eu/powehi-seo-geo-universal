## Evidence Rules

Treat supplied measurements and primary sources as evidence; label observations, inferences, and assumptions separately. For this workflow, never invent missing data or convert an unsupported correlation into causation. Specifically verify every claim used to rédiger un article à partir d’un plan et de sources approuvés. Record the source and observation date for volatile facts.
