Use this prompt when you need to réécrire les pages support pour renforcer une page pilier et éviter la cannibalisation. It is not a generic optimize template: its scope is limited to **supporting-page-alignment** and its output must remain traceable to the supplied evidence.

## Required Inputs

- pillar page.
- supporting pages.
- target intents.
- internal links.
- evidence inventory.

## Evidence Rules

Treat supplied measurements and primary sources as evidence; label observations, inferences, and assumptions separately. For this workflow, never invent missing data or convert an unsupported correlation into causation. Specifically verify every claim used to réécrire les pages support pour renforcer une page pilier et éviter la cannibalisation. Record the source and observation date for volatile facts.

Objective: réécrire les pages support pour renforcer une page pilier et éviter la cannibalisation.
